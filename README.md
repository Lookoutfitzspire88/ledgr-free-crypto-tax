🚀 What is this?

Ledgr is a desktop app that analyzes your trades and generates real-time reports on profit and loss, coin values, realized and unrealized gains, and tax reports — without sending your data anywhere.

Import your transactions from 50+ exchanges, and Ledgr builds a complete picture of your portfolio: what you bought, what you sold, what you still hold, and what you owe.

Your keys. Your coins. Your taxes.

One exe. Everything inside.

💡 Why does it work?

Ledgr runs entirely on your computer. It reads your transaction history (CSV or API), reconstructs every trade, calculates cost basis and gains using FIFO, LIFO, or HIFO, and generates tax reports locally. Nothing leaves your machine.

🔧 How it works

Ledgr imports your transaction history from exchanges (CSV or API) and wallets (address or file). It reconstructs every trade, calculates cost basis using the method you choose, and generates real-time reports on profit and loss, coin values, realized and unrealized gains, and taxes.

Everything happens on your computer. No data is stored or sent to third parties.

🔒 Privacy. Your transactions stay with you. Nothing is uploaded.

❓ FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no hidden payments, no premium features.

Do I need an account?

No. No email, no phone, nothing.

Do I need an API key?

Only if you want to import directly from an exchange. Otherwise you can upload a CSV.

Which exchanges are supported?

50+ exchanges including Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Gate.io, Huobi, Bitfinex, Bitstamp, Gemini, Crypto.com, Robinhood, eToro, MEXC, Bitget, and more.

Which cost basis methods are supported?

FIFO, LIFO, HIFO, and average cost.

Which reports can I generate?

Form 8949, Schedule D, CSV, and PDF — plus real-time P&L, coin values, and realized/unrealized gains.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything is calculated locally on your machine.

Is it safe?

The code is open, the app runs locally, and no data is sent anywhere.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Because the exe is not signed with a digital certificate. This is normal for small open-source projects. Click "More info" → "Run anyway".

Will there be a macOS or Linux version?

Yes, it's planned. Watch the releases.
